# Badge

Small status pill. On this site it carries app status: `default` (green) for
live, `outline` for lab/unreleased.

Variants: `default` · `secondary` · `destructive` · `outline` · `ghost` · `link`

Keep the text to one or two words — the pill is 20px tall and does not wrap.

```tsx
<Badge>Live</Badge>
<Badge variant="outline">In the lab</Badge>
```
