# Leaf Digital — positioning & content architecture

Decided 2026-08-11 with the maintainer. Companion to [BRAND.md](BRAND.md)
(visual kit). This is the source of truth for what the site says and why;
page copy derives from here. Evidence citations at the bottom.

## Essence

We build great products that matter to merchants. Care is the brand: deep
attention to their business, honesty about what our software does, and the
discipline to over-deliver. AI is a tool we use where it creates massive
value — it is not the identity. (The agentic-commerce thesis lives one level
down: in the flagship story and the blog, as insight, not as a costume.)

## Positioning statement (internal)

Leaf is a Shopify product studio that builds software the way merchants wish
everyone built for them: written laws about what it will never touch,
receipts for everything it does, and prices that make sense on the
merchant's side of the ledger.

## The trust pillars (all copy hangs off these)

1. **Written laws.** Every app publishes what it will never do, verbatim,
   before the first install. Care made falsifiable.
2. **Receipts, not promises.** Every change shows before → after and can be
   undone for 30 days. "Never ask for trust — show the receipts."
3. **Actually looked at.** No template output; the quality moat is that the
   work is genuinely done (every image inspected, not title-echoed).
4. **Merchant-ledger pricing.** Anchored to what the work would cost them
   (under an hour of a VA's time), flat, capped, no growth tax.

## Voice rules

- Smart friend who happens to be an expert. The merchant is the hero; we are
  the guide (Gandalf, not Frodo).
- Subject of every sentence: the merchant, their store, their outcome. "We"
  appears only to take responsibility or state a law.
- Buttons are first person ("Scan my store free") — tested pattern.
- Small-but-true numbers over rounded claims (30-day undo, four laws), never
  invented scale ("10,000 merchants") we don't have.
- No corporate filler: if a sentence could open a bank's annual report, cut
  it. No "empower/seamless/revolutionize."

## Locked copy decisions (2026-08-11)

- **H1:** "The work your store needs done — handled, correctly."
  (verbatim voice-of-customer from product research; "without you" cut by
  maintainer decision)
- **Laws section headline:** "It's your store. Software should act like it."
- **Mirror section headline:** "You didn't build your store to babysit it."
- Lead capture = **lab-app waitlists** (no gated lead magnets).
- Services = **aware, not sold**: one consultative line (theme/app builds,
  custom integrations) in the maker section + footer link. Never a peer
  offering next to the apps.
- Frozen strings remain frozen: the four product laws, the 1,847/3,102
  mirror, "we only ever touch the alt field", the receipts line, pricing.

## Other surfaces

- **/apps** — portfolio as one narrative line; lab waitlists repeated.
- **/apps/<app>** — conversion pages (PAS), laws verbatim, pricing honest.
- **Blog** — the authority engine; agentic-commerce insight lives here
  (revive the strongest GEO-era posts as pillar pieces). Newsletter capture.
- **/services** — quiet capability page; consultative tone; no packages.

## Evidence sources (research pass, 2026-08-11)

Clarity vs. cleverness + value-prop structure: MECLABS/MarketingExperiments
test corpus. Dwell time & scanning: NN/g (Weibull dwell-time analysis;
eye-tracking 2010/2018). VoC headlines: Copyhackers Beachway test. Pronouns:
Cruz et al. 2017; Unbounce/Copyhackers button tests. Reviews: Spiegel
Research Center (Northwestern); G2 buyer reports. Trust for unknown vendors:
Stanford/Fogg credibility; NN/g four factors; CXL/Baymard badge research;
Edelman-LinkedIn thought-leadership reports. Teardown set: Basecamp,
37signals, Linear, Panic, Sketch, Tailscale, Fathom, Bear, Hextom, Judge.me,
Boost, Loop, AfterShip, Triple Whale. Folklore explicitly rejected: "one CTA
= +266%", universal question headlines, "benefit-led always wins",
we-we-test as tested law.
