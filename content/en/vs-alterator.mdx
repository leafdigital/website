## What is actually different

**Both apps grade. They grade for different things.**

ALTerator's score is, in its own words, about spotting "SEO improvement areas" —
a number out of 100 for how well a description serves search.

{APP_NAME}'s grade asks a narrower question: **does this sentence describe this
photograph?** Every existing description is sorted into **missing, junk, weak or
good**, and you are shown a vision-checked sample so you can read the verdict
yourself rather than trust a number.

Those overlap, but not completely. A description can be strong for search and
still say nothing a screen-reader user could picture — and a store under the
European Accessibility Act or facing an ADA complaint cares about the second
reading. If your priority is ranking, a 0–100 SEO score is a sensible tool. If it
is whether a customer who cannot see the image knows what they are buying, the
narrower grade is the one that answers it.

Two smaller differences, both from the listings:

- **Undo on the free plan.** Every {APP_NAME} change is undoable for 30 days on
  every plan, free included. ALTerator's history and rollback start on its Basic
  plan; its free plan does not list them.
- **What the free plan is.** ALTerator's free plan is 50 generations, lifetime.
  {APP_NAME}'s is an audit of the whole store, free forever, plus a starter batch
  of descriptions to judge the writing on your own products.
