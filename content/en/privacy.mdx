## The plain version

We run this website and the {APP_NAME} app for Shopify. The website collects
almost nothing. The app reads your product, article, and theme images and their
alt text through Shopify's API, sends images (by URL) with some of your own
catalog text to Anthropic's Claude model to be described or graded, and writes
alt text back — with your approval. We never access, store, or transmit your
storefront customers' personal data: the app doesn't request customer or order
permissions at all. The personal data we do hold is yours — the store owner's
and your staff's — and this policy lists every piece of it. Everything below is
that, in detail.

## The {APP_NAME} app

### What we access

Through the Shopify API, with the permissions you grant at install: your
product images and their alt text, product titles and catalog fields, blog
articles and their images, your published theme's image files, and your store's
language settings. That is the working set. The app does not request access to
customers, orders, or payment data — those permissions are absent by design,
and our build checks fail if a customer or order subscription ever appears.

### What we store

Scan results (coverage grades per image), proposed and applied alt text, and
the before/after history that powers the 30-day undo. We store image references
(Shopify CDN URLs), not copies of your image library.

The personal data the app stores is the merchant's own and their staff's, never
a storefront customer's:

- Your digest email address, if you enable the weekly digest — entered by you,
  used only to send it.
- Shopify staff-user session details (name, email, locale) that Shopify's own
  session tooling attaches when a staff member uses the app. We don't read
  these fields; they're deleted at uninstall and re-swept at redaction.
- Alt text itself, which is free prose. If someone at your store typed personal
  data into an alt text, we store it as typed — we don't inspect it.
- Usage records of the app's own screens and actions, identified by your store
  and, where a session names one, a staff-user ID — never by a customer.

### AI processing — disclosed plainly

To write or grade a description, the app sends your image (as its Shopify CDN
URL) to Anthropic (anthropic.com), whose Claude model returns the text.
Alongside the image, the app sends context from your own store so the
description fits: the product's title and, where set, its description excerpt,
vendor, and type; for blog images, the article's title and summary; for the
quality pass, the alt text currently live on the image. The honest rule: apart
from the image URL, its media type, and your store's language setting,
everything sent is text someone at your store wrote, passed through as typed.
Images and text are transmitted for this purpose only. [Confirm before
publishing: our agreement with Anthropic provides that API data is not used to
train models — state it here once confirmed.] No storefront customer data is
involved at any point: neither flow has anywhere a customer or order could even
be attached.

### Product telemetry

The app records which of its own screens you reached — scan, review, apply — so
we can see where merchants get stuck. It's sent from our servers to PostHog
(posthog.com), identified by your store or a staff-user ID, limited to counts
and states of the app's own interface, and kept for [12 months — confirm
retention setting]. There is no session replay and no screen recording of any
kind — structurally: no recording script is ever served to your browser.
Nothing in telemetry names a customer or contains store content.

### The weekly digest

If you enable it, we send your digest email address and the digest message
through an email delivery provider [name the SMTP vendor once selected]. The
message contains your shop domain, the week's counts, and links back into the
app. Nothing else, and no one else's address.

Those three — Anthropic, PostHog, and the email provider — are the complete
list of third parties this app sends anything to. Everything else the app talks
to is Shopify itself or our own infrastructure.

App data is stored on servers in Germany (Hetzner). Anthropic and PostHog

### Retention and deletion

- **Alt-text change history:** 30 days, to power restore, then pruned.
- **Store data** (scan results, settings, sessions): kept while the app is
  installed. When you uninstall, Shopify sends us a mandatory erasure request
  about 48 hours later, and we erase your store's data when it arrives — the
  deletion is logged, and re-running it removes nothing more because nothing is
  left.
- **Usage and event records:** aged out after 12 months.
- **Billing records:** kept as long as tax and accounting law requires.

### Shopify privacy webhooks

The app implements all three of Shopify's mandatory privacy webhooks. A
customer data request receives a truthful answer: we hold no customer data to
export. A customer redaction request likewise: nothing held, nothing to delete,
and we log the receipt. A shop redaction request erases your store's data as
described above.

## What we never do

We don't sell or share your personal information — under the CCPA's definitions
or anyone else's. We don't run advertising, don't use advertising identifiers,
don't record sessions, and don't touch storefront customer data. Access tokens
for your store are encrypted at rest, and every store's data is isolated from
every other's by construction.
