Alt Text Generator AI has one idea we genuinely like: **its credits never
expire.** Buy a hundred descriptions and they are yours until you use them. For
a store that adds a handful of products a month, that is a better shape of
pricing than a subscription that resets every thirty days.

This page is short, because the listing is short. Everything below is quoted
from it.

## What Alt Text Generator AI actually does

> Generate alt text for all product images using bulk feature

> Generate alt text for individual product images directly.

> Multiple languages are available including English, Spanish, German, and more

The listing says it uses AI; it does not say whether that means looking at the
image or writing from product data, so we will not guess.

## Pricing — read the listing yourself

Its plans, as shown on the pricing cards:

| Plan    | Price           | Credits               |
| ------- | --------------- | --------------------- |
| Free    | Free to install | Buy credits as needed |
| Basic   | $7 / month      | 100                   |
| Pro     | $10 / month     | 500                   |
| Premium | $30 / month     | 1,200                 |

One credit is one description, and the listing says "credits never expire" and
"unused credits roll over."

Two lines on the same listing are worth reading before you buy. The description
says:

> We don't like monthly plans or subscriptions, so all of our plans are one-time
> payment plans.

— while the pricing cards above show each plan as "/ month." And the pricing
section notes:

> External charges may be billed by AltTextGeneratorAI separately from your
> Shopify invoice.

We are not suggesting anything by quoting these; we are quoting them because they
are the two facts a buyer would most want settled, and the listing does not
settle them on its own. Ask the developer which it is before you pay.

{APP_NAME}, for comparison, bills through Shopify: flat monthly prices, charged

## Where it is better

- **Credits that do not expire.** No monthly allowance to lose. If your catalogue
  grows slowly, you pay for exactly what you write.

## What is actually different

**{APP_NAME} grades what you already have before it writes anything.** Every
existing description is sorted into **missing, junk, weak or good**, with a
vision-checked sample you read yourself. Alt Text Generator AI's listing
describes generating alt text; it does not describe checking the alt text you
already have, though it is tagged with Shopify's "SEO score audits" category.

After that, {APP_NAME} reads each image individually, writes what is in the
frame, and holds changes for your approval unless you switch on auto-pilot —
every one undoable for 30 days, on every plan.
