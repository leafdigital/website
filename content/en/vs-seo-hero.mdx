## What is actually different

**SEO HERO starts from your keywords. {APP_NAME} starts from the photograph.**

That is not a quality judgement about SEO HERO; it is a different priority, and
Shopify's own guidance is worth reading on it. Shopify recommends working
keywords in "only where they describe the image, such as product type, color,
material, model name, or use case," and notes that stuffing them in is treated
as a spam signal. Automatic injection can sit on either side of that line
depending on the keyword and the image. The only way to know which side yours
are on is to read them.

{APP_NAME} looks at each image and writes roughly 125 characters describing what
is in the frame — never generated from titles or filenames. Before writing anything, it grades the alt text you already
have as **missing, junk, weak or good**, with a sample you read yourself.

Two smaller differences, from the listings:

- **Undo.** SEO HERO offers "backup and restore your alt text" on its Premium
  ($15) and Premium Plus ($35) plans. {APP_NAME} keeps a 30-day undo on every
  plan, free included.
- **Languages.** SEO HERO's listing is in English and does not say which
  languages it writes in. {APP_NAME} writes in English, German, French, Spanish,
  Italian, Dutch and Portuguese.
