## The two reasons people go looking

A merchant, in a two-star review on AltText.ai's listing, describing the second
one:

> we had some discrepancy issues between the "number of images without alt text"
> and the "number of images resolved" — those values never matched the number of
> actual issues found, or the number of issues actually resolved

That is the whole problem with coverage as a metric, written by somebody who
paid to discover it. A tool that counts filled fields will tell you that you are
done. It cannot tell you whether the field it filled says anything, because it
never graded what was there — and neither does any other alt text app on the
Shopify App Store, including this one until you run the audit.

The first reason is the meter. Another two-star review, and the developer's own
reply underneath it:

> concerned about inflated credit usage

> you just ran out of credits faster than the plan allowed

And a third merchant, on the size of the job:

> we will need several months to work through it

Several months, for a backlog that already exists, on a plan you are already
paying for. That is not a complaint about quality. It is arithmetic.
