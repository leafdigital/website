## The question that matters most

Before choosing, it helps to know how each app decides what to write, because
there are really three answers, and they produce different results on the same
photograph:

- **A template.** You set a pattern — product title, then variant, then type —
  and the app fills it in. Fast, predictable, and identical across every image of
  a product. AltKing and YT SEO work this way.
- **Your product data.** AI turns the title, description and attributes into a
  sentence. More natural than a template, but it can only say what is already in
  the product data. Pixc and Wixpa describe themselves this way.
- **The image itself.** AI looks at the photograph and describes what is in the
  frame. AltText.ai says it does this, SEO HERO says it uses "image context," and
  {APP_NAME} does it for every image.

None of these is wrong. A template is fine for packshots on white. The image
matters for detail shots, on-model photography, lifestyle images and banners —
everything you shot because the title was not enough. Shopify's own guidance,
for what it is worth, is to describe the image and to use keywords "only where
they describe the image."

## The apps

### AltKing — best if you want it free

5.0 across 199 reviews · Built for Shopify · free · launched August 2021

Builds alt text from "simple variables like variants, image names, product
titles, product type, product tags, product brand names," and re-syncs daily so
new images are covered. The most-reviewed dedicated alt text app on this list,
and it costs nothing. One two-star review from September 2025 reports that full
variant support needed the developer's separate Variant Image Automator app; the
listing itself says AltKing "works in sync with Variant Image Automator." [Our full comparison →](/image-voice/vs-altking)

### AltText.ai — best established AI writer

4.5 across 141 reviews · Built for Shopify · from $5 a month · launched May 2023

Its homepage says its AI "automatically analyzes each image on your site," and
its Shopify page says the app uses your product data too. 130+ languages. Metered
by the image — $5 for 100 a month up to $59 for 2,000 — with rollover and extra
credits available.
[Our full comparison →](/image-voice/vs-alttext-ai)

### SEO HERO — best if keywords are the point

4.9 across 171 reviews · Built for Shopify · free for 100, then from $5 a month ·
launched May 2024

Combines "image context + your brand + SEO keywords," with keywords "automatically
injected" and templates you control. If you run a keyword strategy and want it in
every image, this is built for it. Backup and restore start on its $15 plan.
[Our full comparison →](/image-voice/vs-seo-hero)

### StoreSEO — best if you want a whole SEO suite

5.0 across 745 reviews · Built for Shopify · free plan, then from $14.99 a month ·
launched October 2021

Meta titles, schema, sitemaps, indexing requests, image compression, Search
Console — and alt text "in bulk" as one feature among many. Priced by products,
not images. If you need the suite, get the suite. [Our full comparison
→](/image-voice/vs-storeseo)

### Tiny SEO — best if speed matters as much as SEO

5.0 across 2,494 reviews · Built for Shopify · free to install, then from $14 a
month · launched June 2019

An SEO and page-speed suite: image compression, lazy loading, JSON-LD, redirects,
a site audit, and "ALT text & filename optimization." The most-reviewed app on
this page by a wide margin. Its listing does not describe how the alt text is
written.

### ALTerator — best for languages and control

5.0 from 1 review · not Built for Shopify · 50 free generations, then from $5 a
month · launched January 2026

Grades every alt text on a 0–100 SEO score, writes in 18 languages, supports
custom prompts, and keeps a rollback history on paid plans. The closest thing to
{APP_NAME} on this list, and ahead of it on languages. New, with one review.
[Our full comparison →](/image-voice/vs-alterator)

### Pixc — best for very large catalogues

5.0 from 1 review · not Built for Shopify · 25 free a month, then from $8 a month
· launched April 2020

Writes "from your product data" with AI, or from a template, and scans for
"missing or poorly written" alt text. Its $100 plan covers 25,000 images a month —
the lowest price per AI-written image on this list. (For templates, YT SEO is

### YT SEO Optimize Image Alt Text — cheapest template automation

4.6 across 97 reviews · not Built for Shopify · free, or $1.99 a month for daily
automation · launched December 2021

Updates alt text "based on the template you set," with unlimited operations on the
free plan. If a template is what you need, this is about as cheap as it gets.

### Wixpa AI Image Alt Text

4.9 across 12 reviews · Built for Shopify · free to install, credits sold
separately · launched May 2025

Its listing says it "uses your product data to create natural, brand-aligned

### Avada Accessibility — if compliance is the real goal

5.0 across 314 reviews · Built for Shopify · free, then from $9 a month · launched

Not an alt text app first: an accessibility widget with 25+ adjustments, an AI

## How to choose

Three questions, in order:

1. **Do you need anything besides alt text?** If you want schema, sitemaps and
   meta titles too, start with StoreSEO or Tiny SEO and stop here.
2. **Is your photography simple?** If it is mostly packshots on white, a template
   is close to a true description. AltKing is free and well reviewed.
3. **Do you already have alt text?** Many stores do, written by something. Find out whether it describes anything before you pay to write
   more — by hand with the [ten-minute self-audit in our
   guide](/guides/shopify-alt-text), or with an app that grades.

If you want an app to do that last step, [{APP_NAME}'s audit is free](/image-voice)
and writes nothing to your store. If what it finds is fine, you will have learned
that for nothing.
