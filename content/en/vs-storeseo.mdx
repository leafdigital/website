StoreSEO and {APP_NAME} are not really the same kind of app, and pretending
otherwise would waste your time. StoreSEO is a full SEO suite — meta titles,

## What StoreSEO actually does

From its App Store listing:

> Generate meta titles, descriptions, product copy, tags, and image alt text in
> bulk. Compress and resize images to improve store speed. Add JSON-LD and FAQ
> schema, create sitemaps and LLMs.txt, and request indexing. Connect Google
> Search Console and Google Analytics to track SEO performance.

That is a lot of work for one app to take off your hands, and 745 reviews
averaging 5.0 say it does it well. It also carries the Built for Shopify badge.

## Where StoreSEO is better

Nearly everywhere that is not alt text.

- **Scope.** Schema markup, sitemaps, indexing requests, meta titles and
  descriptions, image compression and resizing, keyword tracking, Search Console
  and Analytics. {APP_NAME} does none of those.
- **Track record.** 5.0 across 745 reviews, and the Built for Shopify badge. We
  have no reviews yet and no badge.
- **A real free plan for the whole job.** Its free tier covers 25 products,
  including its image alt text optimizer.

## What is actually different

**In StoreSEO, alt text is one line in a suite. In {APP_NAME}, it is the whole
product.**

That difference is not about quality — StoreSEO's listing does not describe how
its alt text is written, so we will not speculate about it. It is about where the
attention goes. A suite is priced and designed around everything it does; its
plans are measured in products, not images, and the alt text is one of many
things those plans include.

{APP_NAME} is built only around the question of whether each image is described.
It grades what you already have as **missing, junk, weak or good**, with a
vision-checked sample you read yourself. It reads each image individually and
writes what is in the frame. Nothing reaches your store until you approve it —
unless you later switch on auto-pilot — and every change is undoable for 30 days
on every plan. On Curator it covers theme images too —
banners, sliders, section imagery — not just products.

## You can run both

This is the honest answer for a lot of stores. StoreSEO for the SEO suite;
{APP_NAME} for the alt text. They do not do the same job, and the alt text
written by one will simply be graded by the other.

If you already run StoreSEO and are happy with it, the useful question is not
"which app," it is "is the alt text it wrote any good." That is exactly what the
free audit answers.
